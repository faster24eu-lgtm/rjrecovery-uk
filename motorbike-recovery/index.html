Bikes we move

Delivery scooters and 125s, commuter bikes, sports bikes, big tourers and adventure bikes, cruisers, and electric motorbikes. If your bike has been dropped and the bars are bent or the front wheel won’t turn, tell us. It can still be moved.

For delivery riders

If your scooter is your income, every hour matters. Common jobs are a seized engine, a dead battery, a puncture or a broken chain. We take the bike to your home, your dealer or a bike shop that can see it the same day. A courier or rental company might cover the cost, so ask for an invoice.

Theft recoveries

If police have found your stolen bike, you’ll often have to collect it fast, and it often can’t be ridden: the ignition is damaged and the steering lock is broken. We can collect it and take it home or to a garage. Have the police reference and your ID ready.

Questions people ask

How will my motorbike be secured?

On a flatbed or bike trailer, with a wheel chock at the front and soft loops around the bars or frame, so the ratchet straps don’t touch paint or plastics.

Can you recover a bike that’s been dropped or damaged?

Yes. Bent bars, a locked steering head or a flat front tyre can all be handled. Say what you can see when you call.
