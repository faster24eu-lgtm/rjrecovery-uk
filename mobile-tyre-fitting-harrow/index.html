A flat in Harrow

Harrow’s hills and multi-storey car park ramps are hard on tyres and wheels, and locking wheel nuts often seize on cars that only do short local trips. Let us know if you can’t find your locking nut key.

What to tell us when you call

  • Where you are: road, postcode or car park and level
  • Whether you have a spare wheel, a space-saver or only a repair kit
  • Your tyre size from the sidewall, e.g. 225/45 R17 94W
  • Whether you have the locking wheel nut key
  • Whether the wheel itself is damaged: a bent rim, or a tyre torn off the rim

Our options

With a usable spare, the operator fits it and you drive on. With no spare, we either arrange a mobile fitter with your size or tow the car to a tyre shop. At night or with an unusual size, the tow is often quicker. The price is agreed before anyone is sent. See flat tyre help for more detail.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can you fit a new tyre at my home in Harrow?

Often, yes, through a mobile fitter, if your size is in stock. Give us the size when you call and we’ll check before booking.

Is it OK to drive slowly to a tyre shop on a flat?

No. Even a short distance on a flat ruins the tyre and can damage the wheel, unless it’s a run-flat tyre within its stated distance.
