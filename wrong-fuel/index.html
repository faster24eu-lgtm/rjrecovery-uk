Wrong fuel? Don’t turn the key.

Petrol in a diesel is the expensive one. The longer it circulates, the more damage it does to the pump and injectors. If you haven’t started the car, you’ve done the most important thing.

Right now

  1. Don’t start the engine, and don’t even switch the ignition on. On many cars that primes the fuel pump.
  2. Tell the forecourt staff. They’ll help you push the car to a safe spot, put it in neutral and take the handbrake off.
  3. Call us with the filling station name. We’ll get the car off the forecourt and to a garage that can drain and flush the tank.

If you’ve already driven it

Pull over as soon as it’s safe, switch off and don’t restart. Typical signs of petrol in a diesel are knocking, white smoke, losing power and then stalling. With diesel in a petrol car you get misfiring and smoke, and it’s usually less damaging. Either way, the car now needs to be towed, not driven.

Drain at the roadside or at a garage?

Mobile fuel-drain vans exist and can be quicker if you caught it before starting. If you’ve driven on the wrong fuel, a garage should check the fuel system. Tell us exactly what happened and we’ll suggest the cheaper sensible option.

Questions people ask

I put a little petrol in my diesel. Can I just top up with diesel?

It isn’t worth the risk on a modern common-rail diesel. Even a small amount of petrol reduces lubrication in the high-pressure pump. Have it drained.

Does insurance cover wrong fuel?

Some policies and breakdown covers include misfuelling, often with an excess. Check your documents and keep our invoice for a claim.
