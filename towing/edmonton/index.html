Questions people ask

I’ve broken down on the North Circular near Edmonton. What should I do?

Get to the left lane or a slip road if you can, switch on your hazards and get out on the kerb side. If you’re stuck in a live lane, call 999 first, then call us.

Can you collect my car from the IKEA Edmonton car park?

Yes. Give us the car park and roughly where the car is, and let site security know a recovery truck is coming.
