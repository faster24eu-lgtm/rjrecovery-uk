Brent Cross is where the North Circular (A406), the A41 Hendon Way and the start of the M1 come together, next to the shopping centre and the new Brent Cross Town development. A lot of our North London tows start on these junctions.

Where we’re called to in Brent Cross & the A406

  • Brent Cross flyover (A41/A406): Fast, with merging traffic on both levels and nowhere to stop on the flyover itself.
  • Staples Corner (A406/A5/M1 junction 1): A large, busy junction under the flyovers, where the M1 begins.
  • A406 North Circular between Henlys Corner and Staples Corner: Heavy traffic and frequent lane changes.
  • Brent Cross shopping centre car parks and Brent Cross West station: Flat batteries and lost keys after a long shopping trip.

Good to know locally

If your car stops on the A406 or the flyover, don’t try to push it. Hazards on, get out on the kerb side and behind the barrier if there is one, and call 999 if you’re stuck in a live lane. Then tell us which junction you passed last and which way you were heading. Around the Brent Cross Town building sites, roads and access change often, so describe the spot carefully. For Hendon, Colindale and Mill Hill, see our Hendon page.

Getting your car home from Brent Cross

Overnight roadworks and lane closures on the North Circular are common, and they change how a truck reaches you. Tell us exactly where you are and where the car needs to go, and we’ll route the operator around closures. With Brent Cross West station now open on the Thameslink line, more people leave cars in the area; if yours won’t start in a station or shopping car park, give us the level and bay.

FAQ

Questions people ask

I’ve broken down at Staples Corner. What should I do?

Get to the kerb side and somewhere safe, hazards on. If you’re in a live lane and can’t move, call 999. Then call us with the road and your direction.

Can you collect my car from the Brent Cross shopping centre?

Yes. Tell us the car park and the level, and let centre security know a recovery truck is coming.
